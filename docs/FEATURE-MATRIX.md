# Feature matrix

**Village-to-Global** adds sample hierarchical discovery, supplier and product passports, offer comparisons, Connect estimates and storefront drafting. A connected supplier directory, live quotation delivery, geospatial radius search and private verification are pending.

| Module | Working demo path | Coverage still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Shop | Search, category and sort, product passport, gallery, variant cart, wishlist, comparison, simulated order | Multiple verified offers per product, dynamic prices, richer reviews and shipments |
| Nearby | City selection, optional geolocation, sample shops, distance filters, maps and stock-check lifecycle | Shop photos, verified inventory and integrated contact or directions |
| Global Atlas | Eleven sourcing cities, two sample manufacturer profiles, country and category filters, map, MOQ and tiers, sample RFQ | Full geographic hierarchy, supplier comparisons and customs estimation |
| Together | Join, leave, update units, threshold pricing, expiry and local group drafts | Shareable groups, participation by multiple users and committed group orders |
| Care | Six service types, slot booking and cancellation, repair cost assistant | Provider calendar, profile verification and service review workflow |
| Connect | Validated request, sample quotes, accept/reject status and counteroffer notes | File uploads, per-quote lifecycle, cross-user delivery and persistent conversation threads |
| Business | Profile, product/service drafts, local publish state, order and booking status, count summaries | Storefront publishing, item editing and stock movements, seller-originated quotes, verification and role permissions |
| Account | Local profile, settings, addresses, wishlist, cart, orders, bookings and activity | Authentication, account recovery and sync |
| Admin | Local activity summary | Authenticated administration and audit trail |

The optional connected Foundation build extends Accounts, Business and Global Atlas with verified email accounts, one-or-many business memberships, Seller Studio, Trust Passports and operator moderation. Customer-facing Pages remains Demo Mode. See [setup](MARKETPLACE-FOUNDATION.md).
