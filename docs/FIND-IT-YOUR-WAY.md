# Find It Your Way

Use **Find a product** on Home or **Explore buying options** from Search or a Shop product. Provide a keyword and expand the optional criteria for quantity, location, deadline, budget, or specifications. Review ready-made results, Nearby stock options, or sourcing offers. In the public demo, catalogue and sourcing terms are illustrative.

Try **Wireless headphones**, **handmade baskets**, or **custom machined shaft coupling**. The basket search can broaden to baskets while retaining quantity and deadline. A stock check requires a supported product and the configured stock pilot. The browser demo supports review and save of a custom sourcing request, plus existing Saved Searches and Quote Comparison.

See the [intent model](BUYER-INTENT-MODEL.md), [matching rules](BUYING-OPTIONS-MATCHING.md), and [handoff map](BUYER-HANDOFFS.md).
