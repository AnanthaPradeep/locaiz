# PLP competitor research

Amazon documents category-specific browse refinements and a product hierarchy separate from universal search. LOCAIZ keeps both routes and uses ten departments with shared product identities. [Amazon browse tree guidance](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1661?locale=en)

Baymard's usability research distinguishes OR matching within a filter from AND matching across filters. Its applied-filter research recommends making active choices visible. LOCAIZ applies those rules to repeated URL values and exposes dismissible filters. [Multi-value filtering research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/allow-applying-of-multiple-filter-values) · [Applied filter research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/how-to-design-applied-filters)

Alibaba distinguishes direct purchase from supplier enquiries for custom items and terms such as MOQ. LOCAIZ keeps retail checkout and quote-only manufacturing separate and scopes supplier filters to the same offer. [Alibaba buyer guidance](https://helpcenter.alibaba.com/s/buyer/knowledge?categoryId=275875306&m_station=buyer&questionId=1beca5c5cc5643fca1a2ca6919c13c34sgvpc278077001)
