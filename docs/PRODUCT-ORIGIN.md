# Product origin and offers

A product keeps a stable catalogue ID, category, claimed origin, manufacturing method, variant and specifications. Offers reference it by ID and carry independent supplier, purchase method, currency, minimum quantity, availability and fulfilment terms.

Use the product passport to compare offers for a single canonical product. A wholesaler can ship a sample product from a different place than where it was made. A retailer's pickup location says where a customer could collect an item and conveys no origin claim.

Sample claims are always labelled. The connected schema distinguishes seller-reported, reviewed and independently verified provenance. Certifications and independent evidence need their own review process before display.
