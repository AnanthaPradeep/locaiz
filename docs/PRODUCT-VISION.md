# Product vision

LOCAIZ helps a buyer find a product locally, order a retail item, identify a wholesale market, consolidate buying with a group or repair an existing item. Search is the common starting point. Product passports and quote comparisons make provenance and cost visible. Seller tools serve retailers, manufacturers, traders and service professionals.

The demo focuses on testing these paths with a small fixture catalogue and independent LOCAIZ branding. Success in a connected pilot should be measured by completed searches, verified supplier matches and fulfilled requests.
