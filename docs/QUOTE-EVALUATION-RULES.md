# Quote evaluation rules

Every quote uses the requested quantity. If quantity is below MOQ, the quote stays visible and no subtotal is computed. Otherwise, apply the highest price tier up to the requested quantity. Monetary arithmetic uses safe integers in original currency minor units. Known costs include items and quoted fees. A complete quoted total requires confirmed destination coverage and explicit terms for every applicable cost. Different currencies are shown separately.

Production time and transit time are separate. A missing transit estimate prevents a complete delivery estimate. If production alone exceeds the available days, show Likely late. Pickup can be offered even when stock is unconfirmed. Eligibility and cost completeness are separate statuses. Expired quotes remain visible and cannot be selected.
