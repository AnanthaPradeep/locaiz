# Sourcing flows

1. Browse the directory by locality, category, role or purchase method.
2. Open a supplier or product passport. Compare minimum quantities and applicable unit tiers in their original currencies.
3. Open a quote or custom-order enquiry through Connect. The selected offer supplies the product and starting quantity. Enter the destination, date, budget and specifications and indicate whether a sample or customisation is needed.
4. Open the saved requirement and compare sample offers that support the product, MOQ, delivery country and customisation choice.

The current Connect sourcing flow stores its enquiry on the device. Estimates are calculated from sample offer prices. Shipping, customs, tax, sample cost, delivery dates and quote validity require confirmation. Exchange rates and cross-border payment are outside this release.

The [stock pilot](NEARBY-STOCK-CONFIRMATION.md) remains the separate authenticated flow for availability. Custom products use a sourcing enquiry.
