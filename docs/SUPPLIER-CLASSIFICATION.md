# Supplier classification

The taxonomy includes Artisan, Home-based producer, Cottage industry, Workshop, Small manufacturer, Medium manufacturer, Large manufacturer, Wholesaler, Distributor, Retailer, Exporter and Service provider. A supplier can have several roles.

In **Business → Create supplier storefront**, the demo wizard collects basic identity, business and manufacturing locations, capacity and speciality, a catalogue example, and fulfilment terms. The review step saves a browser-local draft in Business Inventory. Users should enter fictional data. Real identity documents, public publication and reviewer decisions require an authorised backend.

The database distinguishes unverified, identity reviewed, documents reviewed and factory reviewed. Reviewing and public publication require a trusted operator. Business membership alone cannot promote either field.
