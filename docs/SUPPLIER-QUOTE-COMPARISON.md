# Supplier quote comparison

Open **My Requirements** and choose **Try demo quote comparison** for the 50-basket scenario. Choose two or three offers and select **Compare quotes**. Each comparison uses one quantity, destination, date and customisation choice. The desktop table scrolls horizontally on small screens with field labels pinned. Use **Show differences only** to narrow the table.

Shortlists and clarification drafts are local to the requirement. Selecting a quote records the choice and its warnings. The action creates neither a purchase nor a payment and does not contact the supplier. The four sample offers include MOQ mismatch, pending shipping, late delivery, and an expired export quote in USD. The Global Atlas comparison reuses the sample product's offer set, including incompatible offers.
